Idea I used today: "graceful failure" — every error becomes a short human sentence with a next step (for example "Cannot reach the assistant. Is the backend running?"), and when the assistant does not know it shows who can help (the Training Office) instead of guessing.

Idea I would add: let the user correct a wrong answer, not only rate it 👍/👎, for example by adding a short comment box after 👎 so the team learns what was wrong.
